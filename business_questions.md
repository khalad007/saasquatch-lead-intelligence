# Caprae Capital Handbook — Business Understanding Answers

### What is Caprae's Mission?

Caprae Capital positions itself differently from a typical private equity firm: rather than
relying mainly on financial engineering, its stated approach is to grow the businesses it acquires
through hands-on operational and technological support after the deal closes. The firm frames M&A
as a multi-year value-creation journey rather than a one-time transaction — the real work happens
in the years after acquisition, not at the moment of it. Practically, this means building internal
tools (like the SaaSquatch lead-gen platform this project extends) and AI-driven processes that
portfolio companies can use directly, rather than just advising from the outside.

### Why do you want to work at Caprae Capital?

I want to work somewhere my code has a direct, visible effect on whether a real business succeeds,
rather than being one more contributor on a large team shipping incremental features. Caprae's
model — where engineers build tools that get used inside actual portfolio companies, not just
internal dashboards — matches how I've worked over the past few years: I've owned features
end-to-end at Turnkey ISP (from database design through deployment) and built several AI-powered
tools independently outside of work because I wanted to understand how AI could be applied
practically, not just as an API call. Caprae's emphasis on finding talent before it's on the
resume and its bias toward people who take ownership is the kind of environment I do my best
work in.

### How is Caprae Changing the ETA Space and Broader PE?

Most search funds and small-PE acquirers rely on generalist operators and outside consultants to
improve a target company post-close. Caprae's differentiator is building its own software rather
than licensing third-party SaaS tools for portfolio companies. That's a meaningfully different
model in the ETA space: instead of value creation depending entirely on one operator's hustle,
Caprae is trying to productize the improvements it makes — AI adoption, sales tooling, process
automation — so they're repeatable across every company in the portfolio.
